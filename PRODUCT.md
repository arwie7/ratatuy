# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React + Tailwind CSS + shadcn, with Magic UI components for motion (user's choice, 2026-10-14). Static landing, runs locally first; hosting not decided.

## Users

Two audiences in Ulyanovsk, with different motives and budgets:

- People organising a celebration: birthday, anniversary, corporate party, children's party, possibly a wedding. They compare halls by capacity, conditions and dates, then call or leave a request.
- Families with children coming for an ordinary lunch or dinner. They want the kids occupied so the adults can eat in peace.

## Product Purpose

Landing page for the restaurant complex «Рататуй» (ratatuycafe.ru). Its job is to turn a visit into a hall booking request or a call, and secondarily into a table visit or a delivery order. Success: more banquet requests and calls, fewer guests confused about what the place offers.

## Positioning

A restaurant where children are part of the plan: a glass playroom with a maze, toys, cartoons and the «Игрёнок» terminal in the main hall, and a separate children's banquet hall with its own maze and dance floor. Three halls of different size cover anything from a family dinner to a 60-guest celebration.

## Brand Commitments

- Name «Рататуй» (footer: «Рататуй Кафе»). The current site has a round grey badge logo («Ресторанный комплекс», «основан в 2014», «Belki Family», script «Рататуй») at ratatuycafe.ru/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/ratatuj-logo_end_v-shriftah-2-*.png; the analysis calls it grey and easy to miss. Whether to keep, redraw or replace it is the owner's decision.
- Tone: plain, warm, factual. No superlatives («лучший», «идеальное место»); say «проверенные» instead of «лучшие» for contractors.
- The user explicitly bans AI-looking decoration: bullet dots before paragraphs, long em dashes, decorative section numbers (01, 02, 03).

## Product Principles

1. Every claim on the page is one the restaurant has already made; unknowns stay out rather than get invented.
2. Booking a hall is the primary action and is never more than one tap away, on mobile included.
3. Children are the differentiator; show the playroom and the children's hall as concretely as the halls themselves.
4. Separate the banquet path from the delivery path so neither confuses the other.
